FREELANCE SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT

Business software that reflects how the work actually gets done.

I help turn spreadsheets, manual handoffs and fragile legacy processes into applications with clear rules, useful reporting and a maintainable technical foundation.

HOW I HELP

Make the next technical decision a useful one.

Business software needs to be designed around the people who operate it. Before coding, I map the workflow, decisions, data ownership, exceptions and reporting needs. That avoids producing software that merely moves the same confusion into a browser.

  • Workflow discovery and planningClarify roles, process steps, edge cases and the smallest useful delivery sequence.
  • Application deliveryBuild web or desktop business software, database-backed workflows, dashboards and connected internal tools.
  • Long-term ownershipDeploy, monitor, improve and maintain the application as the business changes.

I work directly with clients through planning and implementation. The result can be a focused internal tool or a larger application, but the success measure remains the same: work becomes easier to run and safer to change.
